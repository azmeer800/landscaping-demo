Consult

We walk your garden together, listen to how you want to use it, and measure up - free of charge.

02

You receive a scaled plan, planting list and one fixed written price. No allowances, no surprises.

🍁 This season's garden note

Autumn is the best planting season in Tennessee - warm soil and cool air let roots settle before summer stress. If you are planning new trees, shrubs or a lawn renovation, a quote this month means planting at exactly the right time.
